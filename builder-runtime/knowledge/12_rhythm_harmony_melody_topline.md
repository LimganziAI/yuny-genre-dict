## MUST APPLY
**Contour = blueprint, harmony = ground, cues = construction — they ship together or Suno averages them.**

**3-layer contour notation (prose contour BANNED):**
A) Formal tag opens the section in LYRIC: [Ascending melody] [Descending melody] [Emotional climax] [Flattened tone] [Falling tension].
B) Harmony link in CREATE prompt: "hook leaps to the 5th of [chord], ascending then steps down" — leaps LAND on chord tones.
C) One degree-line aid where stakes are high ("verse hovers 1-3, chorus peaks on 6").

**Voice-leading laws:** leap → chord-tone landing; stepwise may pass; after a leap, step back the other way; ONE peak note per song, on a strong beat, usually final chorus; tendency tones resolve.

**Section harmony plan (CREATE carries it, per section, non-diatonic named):** verse stable diatonic → pre-chorus tension (ii-V motion, pedal, or chromatic walk) → chorus lift via borrowed color (bVII, bIII, IV-iv, or modal mixture) → bridge = the harmonic turn (new root area or relative shift) → final chorus may key-up or re-voice.

**Hook memorability levers:** repetition with ONE distinctiveness spike — unusual interval at the hook, syncopation that feels new, or the borrowed chord hitting exactly under the title phrase. Hook = lyrical+melodic+rhythmic combo, engineered, not hoped.

**Topline reality:** key/tempo/chords come from the track; vocal lives in the mids — arrangement leaves it room (card 13 zones); vocal rhythm locks to or pushes against the groove deliberately, never floats.

**Groove vocabulary in prompts:** name the pocket ("laid-back behind-the-beat pocket", "driving on-top eighths", "swung 16ths", "half-time chorus") — BPM alone is not a feel.

**Realization cues:** rise → (building intensity)+crescendo+belt; fall → (stripped back)+decrescendo; plateau → deadpan, conversational; leap → (belted)+peak marker.

# FINAL ADDENDUM — Vocal-Harmony-Melody Triplet

Every serious song checks the triplet:
1 vocal posture
2 harmonic color
3 melody contour

Examples:
- light chest-mix belt → forward 4th/5th leap, one peak, chord-tone landing, open-vowel sustain
- airy head voice → stepwise contour, suspended/maj7/9 color, fewer hard consonant peaks
- speech-tone indie → narrow verse range, rhythmic motif, bass-motion pressure
- thick chest belt → lower peak, slower harmonic rhythm, arrangement drop before peak

If melody/harmony feels generic, fetch harmony/rhythm fullbody from archive before writing new prompts.

# PRECISION ADDENDUM — VOCAL MONEY ZONE

For serious builds, choose key/tonal color so the chorus peak sits inside the lead vocal’s money zone.
A dramatic peak outside the vocal comfort zone is not impact; it causes strain, gender drift, or weak render.
One peak note per song remains mandatory, usually in the final chorus.
## CURRENT ADDENDUM — Technical Harmony Prompting
When useful, include chord/progression language and hook landing behavior in CREATE. This is not optional decoration for serious builds; it prevents generic toplines. Name the tonal color, borrowed lift, or hook drop if it defines the song.
For technical prompts, keep the listener result readable: code/progression supports the vocal money zone and one-peak rule, never replaces them.
