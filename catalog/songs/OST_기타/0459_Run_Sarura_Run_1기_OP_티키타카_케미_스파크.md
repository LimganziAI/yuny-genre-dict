*Style/Prompt (tags):*
```
A bright, sunny Pop-Punk/Power-Pop anthem for a slice-of-life anime. Vibe: relentlessly fun, energetic, and lighthearted, like skateboarding on a sunny day.

The music is a live band sound focused on creating maximum space for vocals. Drums: simple, fast, and bouncy 4-beat punk rhythm. Bass: bright, played with a pick for a rhythmic, bouncy feel. Electric Guitar: use a bright, simple tone with driving power chords, avoiding heavy distortion or complex riffs. The mix must be clean and spacious.

The vocal direction is critical: feature multiple, distinct female vocalists cast for clear variety in Vocal Tone, Singing Technique (창법), Style Influence, and Weirdness. Vocalists must change frequently, often every couplet, for a fast-paced, conversational feel. The structure is dynamic: a powerhouse vocalist builds the pre-chorus; the chorus is a massive tutti anthem; the bridge is a stripped-down break for expressive vocals; climax with a final chorus, ending on a sharp band hit.
```
