# SIM-20260612 LYRIC GENERATION STRATEGY ROUND 3

## purpose
Compare writing strategies for Korean lyric generation against the team corpus craft bar.

## test scenario
A speaker waits in a laundromat after work. The dryer keeps turning with clothes that are not fully dry. The speaker is trying not to text someone, but the repeated dryer motion makes the relationship feel unfinished.

## target lane
Narrative Korean indie pop / R&B with sentence hook. Team-corpus inspired, not copied.

## strategy A — explanatory thesis-first
Method:
Start from the emotional thesis and explain it clearly.

Result tendency:
- easy to understand
- too flat
- chorus becomes message summary
- objects are symbolic labels rather than actions
- low replay value

Example behavior:
The lyric says the relationship is unfinished instead of letting the dryer, sleeve, coin, screen, and waiting body prove it.

Verdict:
Fail for team corpus craft bar. Useful only as hidden monologue, not final lyric.

## strategy B — poetic image-first
Method:
Start from dense imagery and metaphor.

Result tendency:
- more lyrical surface
- risk of decorative abstraction
- speaker posture weak
- objects may float without action
- chorus can sound pretty but not personal

Example behavior:
Dryer becomes planet, moon, fog, memory, etc. without the speaker handling anything.

Verdict:
Partial fail. Some images useful, but final lyric needs action and posture.

## strategy C — scene-action-speaker first
Method:
Start from what the speaker does, notices, avoids, repeats, and lies about.

Craft brief:
```text
SONG AS A SCENE: laundromat after work, dryer keeps adding time
SPEAKER POSTURE: pretending to be practical, actually waiting for an excuse
EMOTIONAL ENGINE: unfinished drying equals unfinished contact
OBJECTS WITH ACTIONS: coin inserted, sleeve checked, screen locked, chair warmed, lint removed, timer extended
CENTRAL METAPHOR SOURCE: clothes keep turning but do not dry; the speaker keeps stopping but does not leave
CHORUS IDENTITY: sentence contradiction — I came to dry clothes, but I keep drying the same excuse
V2 ANGLE CHANGE: the speaker notices someone else's laundry finished and gone
FINAL SHIFT: the speaker stops adding coins, but still stays until the drum cools
VOCABULARY PALETTE: dryer, coin, sleeve, screen, chair, lint, timer, warm air, door, hand
LINE LOGIC: plain action lines carry image pressure
```

Result tendency:
- situation recoverable
- speaker posture visible
- objects have verbs
- metaphor grows from scene
- chorus identity clear
- V2 can change angle naturally
- final shift is small but real

Verdict:
Pass direction. This should be the default for narrative team-corpus-style Korean lyrics.

## sample chorus direction from strategy C
Do not use as final locked lyric. Direction only:

```text
옷은 아직 덜 말랐고
나는 핑계를 한 번 더 넣어
문자는 끝내 안 보냈는데
손은 자꾸 따뜻해져
```

Assessment:
- understandable Korean
- objects and actions connected
- central contradiction visible
- could be improved for sharper hook identity

## improved hook target
```text
말리러 온 건 옷인데
자꾸 내가 여기 남아
```

Assessment:
- stronger sentence hook
- easier to repeat
- metaphor clear without explaining everything
- fits team-corpus craft bar better

## strategy decision
For narrative Korean lyrics, YUNY should use:

```text
hidden monologue → scene-action-speaker craft brief → chorus identity → V2 angle → final shift → line drafting
```

Do not use:

```text
theme explanation → line trimming
```

Do not use:

```text
image pile → fake poetic lyric
```

Card 05/06 should explicitly prefer scene-action-speaker generation for narrative team-corpus-style Korean lyrics.
