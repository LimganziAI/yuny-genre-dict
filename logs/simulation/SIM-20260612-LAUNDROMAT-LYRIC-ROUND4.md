# SIM-20260612 LAUNDROMAT LYRIC ROUND 4

## purpose
Run a lyric-only simulation using the current producer-first Korean lyric craft runtime.

## scenario
After work, the speaker waits in a laundromat. The dryer keeps turning, but the clothes are still damp. The speaker is trying not to text someone. The unfinished dryer cycle becomes a scene-born metaphor for not leaving the relationship.

## craft brief
```text
SONG AS A SCENE: late-night laundromat after work, dryer adds time
SPEAKER POSTURE: practical on the surface, waiting for an excuse underneath
EMOTIONAL ENGINE: drying clothes becomes delay, delay becomes contact temptation
OBJECTS WITH ACTIONS: coin inserted, sleeve checked, lint peeled, phone locked, chair warmed, timer extended, door opened then closed
CENTRAL METAPHOR SOURCE: things keep turning but do not finish
CHORUS IDENTITY: sentence contradiction
V2 ANGLE CHANGE: another customer’s laundry finishes and leaves
FINAL SHIFT: speaker stops adding coins but stays until the drum cools
VOCABULARY PALETTE: coin, sleeve, dryer, timer, lint, screen, chair, door, warm air, hand
LINE LOGIC: plain action lines carry image pressure
```

## expression comparison on worst candidates

### candidate 1
```text
손은 화면 위에서 식질 않아
```
Issue: poetic but slightly unnatural. Hands do not normally “cool” as naturally as heat or screen/body. Intended meaning: the hand remains warm/delayed over the phone.

Repair options:
```text
손은 화면 위에 한참 남아
손은 아직 화면 위에 머물러
손끝만 오래 식지 않았어
```
Decision:
Use `손끝만 오래 식지 않았어` if keeping warmth metaphor; use `손은 화면 위에 한참 남아` for plainer Korean.

### candidate 2
```text
조금 더 늦어도 되는 사람처럼
```
Issue: understandable but slightly abstract. Needs clearer action relation.

Repair options:
```text
조금 더 늦어질 핑계를 얻은 사람처럼
집에 늦어도 되는 이유가 생긴 것처럼
```
Decision:
Use shorter line if melody needs breath; use prose repair for narrative clarity.
