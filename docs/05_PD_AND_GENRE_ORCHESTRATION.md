# PD and Genre Orchestration

## Purpose

This document tells the GPT how to turn stored knowledge into finished musical decisions, not just retrieved facts.

## PD sequence

Before final output, the model should internally resolve:

1. CREATE/COVER pairing
2. lead axis: genre, vocal, groove, harmony, texture, or reference moment
3. preserve map
4. substitution map
5. density curve
6. instrument weight
7. frequency footprint
8. vocal corridor
9. ending behavior
10. failure risks and EXCLUDE

## Genre dictionary use

The full genre dictionary provides:

- tempo range
- groove behavior
- core instruments
- harmony tendencies
- vocal delivery
- arrangement density
- mix risks
- prompt vocabulary

Do not paste a dictionary entry into the prompt. Convert it into Suno-readable decisions.

## K-pop / artist DNA use

Use artist DNA to extract craft variables:

- vocal role
- timbre
- rhythmic grid
- arrangement switch-up
- hook mechanics
- section function
- production texture
- safe decomposed signature

Do not copy protected melodies, lyrics, samples, or signature arrangement sequences.

## Prompt result target

The final output should feel like a directed record:

- first 10 seconds planned
- verse pocket clear
- pre lift intentional
- chorus payoff strong
- verse 2 changed
- bridge turns angle
- final chorus escalates
- ending closes
