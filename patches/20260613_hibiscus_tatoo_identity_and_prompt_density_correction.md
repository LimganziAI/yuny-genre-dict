Tatoo is female. `Sis Tatoo`, `시스타투`, and `타투` are the same character/vocal family, not separate people.

Do not treat the previous male/noir line as gender truth. It is only one possible low/noir rhythmic delivery mode inside the female Tatoo family.

Use `knowledge-evolving/vocal-palette/characters/tatoo.md` as the corrected source.

For `히비스커스`, use Mode B/C hybrid. The key discovery was that raising the vocal center kept the melody from dropping and made the render closer to Tatoo. Do not drag the whole song downward to achieve dryness.

## Prompt density failure

The session failed not only in concept relock but also in prompt craftsmanship:

- CREATE/COVER were too short and generic compared with current Suno practice.
- Range anchors such as soprano / mezzo-soprano / alto were not used when they would have stabilized the render.
- Vocal natural-language acting was too thin.
- Instrument articulation was under-specified.
- Cues were descriptive instead of renderable commands placed between lines.
- COVER did not read like the final record: preserve map, timing/rest map, vocal identity, substitution/refine map, quality stack, final/outro protection were underbuilt.
- EXCLUDE included discarded meeting ideas instead of actual failure classes.

## Required runtime behavior

When a concept is relocked and lyrics are mostly fixed:

1. Treat the fixed lyric as source material, not a finished package.
2. Rebuild CREATE from current concept, not from old fields.
3. Rebuild COVER as the final record, not a copy of CREATE.
4. Fill prompt budget with execution value: range anchor, vocal acting, melody firewall, instrument articulation, section energy, mix corridor, and quality stack.
5. Add lyric cues after the lyric passes, using renderable devices such as rests, drops, held space, harmony entry, band cut, or instrumental motif.
6. EXCLUDE only actual failure classes: wrong vocal style, delivery drift, mix defects, source-skin drag, language drift, lyric timing drift.

## Lyric process lessons

The strongest lyric improvements came from micro-editing and subtraction:

- Shorten quoted speech: `히비스커스 마실래` → `마실래?`
- Remove explanatory bridge line: `속으로 한 번 부르면`
- Remove summary connector: `그런 것들이`
- Use open sensation over explanation: `말보다 늦은 반짝임만 / 테이블 위에 머물렀어`
- Delete meter-breaking scenic line when the song grid needs it.
- Preserve object logic: skirt hem, phone light, thumb, cup red, white hand, bridge word, sourness ending.
