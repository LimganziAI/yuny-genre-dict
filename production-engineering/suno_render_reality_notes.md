# Suno Render Reality Notes — CURRENT

Use as operational risk notes, not immutable law.

## Observed / research-supported risks
- Suno generates music from text/audio prompts and can include vocals and instruments.
- Recent models improve fidelity, separation, and arrangement complexity, but prompts for era/genre/effects can still be misread.
- Vocal direction can be sanitized: too much reverb, automatic harmonies, perfect pitch, reduced emotional edge.
- Cover/final transforms should be treated as conditional generation from source audio, not as a fresh song.
- Metatags are steering attempts; they should be supported by structure, prompt design, and lyrics, not trusted alone.

## Practical policy
- Use structural cues over typography.
- Use [ ] for non-sung events and ( ) for audible ad-libs.
- [Whispered] only after a drop/silence and on a short phrase.
- [Held] on open vowels only and rarely.
- Asterisks/bold are secondary; pair with [Sudden drop], [One-bar rest], [Band drops out], [Kick-only break], [Harmony enters], or [Vocal doubles only on final phrase].
- Avoid toy-synth vocabulary unless the concept wants it.
