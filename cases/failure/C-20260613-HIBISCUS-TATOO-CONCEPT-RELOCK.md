## Primary failures
### 1. Concept relock failure
The assistant kept patching old fields after major changes. Once vocal, genre, lyric density, and listening judgment changed, it should have stopped and rebuilt from a new current lock.

### 2. Vocal / melody firewall failure
User requested vocal tone only. The assistant let low-energy / behind-the-beat / monotone / spoken-like descriptors reshape the melody and song feel.
Correct split:
- MELODY LOCK: topline contour, hook lift, phrase lengths, chorus shape, peak note, lyric timing.
- VOCAL TONE ONLY: timbre, range color, mic distance, diction, phrase attack, breath/fry, acting.

### 3. Scope-lock failure
A vocal-tone repair drifted into tempo/arrangement changes. That violates vocal-only scope unless approved.

### 4. Prompt density failure
The CREATE/COVER prompts were too short/generic compared with current Suno practice. They lacked range anchors, natural-language acting, instrument articulation, section motion, quality stack, and current-concept rebuild.

### 5. COVER underbuild
COVER was treated as a cleaner copy instead of the final record. It lacked preserve map, timing/rest protection, vocal identity preservation, substitution/refine map, quality stack, and final/outro protection.

### 6. EXCLUDE contamination
Discarded brainstorm words were carried into EXCLUDE. EXCLUDE should target actual failure classes, not old meeting history.

### 7. Lyric performance grid failure
A literary draft was used as a song script before section grid, breath, rests, quoted asides, and bridge compression were fully rebuilt.
