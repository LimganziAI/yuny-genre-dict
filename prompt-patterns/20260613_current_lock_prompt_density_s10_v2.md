# Prompt Pattern V2 — Current Lock Rebuild and Dense Suno Fields

Date: 2026-06-13
Source case: Hibiscus / Tatoo

## Trigger
Use when:
- user says prompts are lazy or underbuilt
- concept changed after listening
- lyrics are mostly fixed but sound, vocal, or COVER failed
- user asks for current Suno prompt quality
- old brainstorm terms leak into EXCLUDE

## Current lock pass
Before final fields, state internally:
- FIXED: lyrics, title, melody, tempo, character, cues
- CHANGED: vocal, sound, mix, cover strategy
- DISCARDED: old brainstorm concepts
- DO NOT TOUCH: melody, lyric timing, section order, arrangement
- OBSERVED FAILURES: exact render defects
- CONFIRMATION NEEDED: if any

## CREATE density checklist
1. microgenre plus scene and finish
2. BPM, feel, meter
3. key or tonal color
4. vocal range anchor
5. vocal acting in natural language
6. melody contour and hook behavior
7. 3 or 4 instruments with articulation and register
8. signature motif
9. section movement and energy arc
10. mix corridor if important

## COVER density checklist
1. target function: refine, remaster, or transform
2. preserve map: melody, lyric timing, section order, rests, final and outro
3. do not change map
4. substitution or refine map
5. vocal identity protection
6. timing and rest protection
7. quality stack
8. final and outro preservation

## Cue pass
After lyric survives, add renderable cues:
- One beat rest
- Two beat rest with piano only
- Band softens
- Drums cut
- Harmony enters only on final phrase
- Instrumental Outro: motif fades

## EXCLUDE purity
Good targets:
- wrong delivery: talk singing, flat narration, rushed vocal
- vocal defects: buried lead, harsh sibilance, whisper only, robotic autotune
- mix defects: muddy low mids, harsh cymbals, mono collapse
- source skin drag if observed
Bad targets:
- genre mentioned then abandoned
- obvious absence with no drift risk
- generic negative spam
