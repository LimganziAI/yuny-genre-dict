# 06 Korean Lyric Prosody and Hook — Korean Language, Image, Morpheme, Phoneme Runtime

## CURRENT — Mode-Aware Korean Lyric Gate

Korean lyric quality is not one style. This card checks whether the chosen lyric mode works in Korean. Conversational lines, poetic images, surreal fragments, chants, bilingual hooks, and minimal residues pass by different standards.

## role
Controls Korean naturalness, literary/poetic function, spoken register, josa/eomi acting, subject economy, image grounding, repetition, phoneme/syllable singability, and lyric repair gates.

## priority order
1. Mode truth: the lyric behaves like its declared mode.
2. Voice/stance truth: speaker, persona, chorus, or image-system has a stable relation.
3. Korean sentence or fragment validity: complete sentences when needed; intentional fragments when mode supports them.
4. Section function: line belongs here, not anywhere.
5. Morpheme/register fit.
6. Phoneme/breath/topline fit.
7. Beauty, novelty, compression.

A line may be plain. A line may be strange. It may not be dead.

## mode-specific pass standards

### conversational/narrative
Good lines sound chosen by a person in a situation.
Need: recoverable scene, relation, body/action/object, subject economy, pressure, line handoff.
Fails: theme summary, translated English logic, slogan hook, polished but unquotable line.

### poetic/symbolic
Good lines build an image relation.
Need: object + time/body/background + pressure; silence; return; cut; final residue.
Fails: decorative metaphor, abstract emotion label, poem-like fog with no relation.

### surreal/artful
Good lines obey an internal rule.
Need: concrete anchor, recurrence, controlled strangeness, one emotional readable thread.
Fails: random symbol chain, image salad, cleverness with no pressure.

### chant/anthem
Good lines are repeatable in a group mouth.
Need: strong vowel/coda, call-response, repeat dose, one semantic spike, section energy.
Fails: slogan without groove, too many concept nouns, no variation.

### vocable/phonetic
Good lines work as sound.
Need: vowel map, percussive consonants, rhythmic cell, body feel, tiny semantic frame.
Fails: random lalala, hard coda on held note, no groove link.

### bilingual
Good lines work section-wise.
Need: one section one language, shared vowel seam, line/breath map, retained fragments only when functional.
Fails: mid-section language salad, English phonetics leaking into Korean vocal.

### theatrical/character
Good lines act.
Need: role pressure, quotable gesture, listener relation, stage timing, contrast.
Fails: lore dump, narrator explanation, character name replacing voice.

### minimal/fragment
Good lines make silence active.
Need: few words, loaded object, return with change, breath/rest architecture.
Fails: underwritten placeholder, cues carrying all emotion.

## Korean line quality
A strong Korean lyric line may use:
- subject deletion
- flexible word order
- small repair words under pressure
- repeated sound or ending when intentionally musical
- fragment when context supplies the rest
- literary noun-ending or cut when mode supports it

Reject fake naturalness:
- random fillers pasted everywhere
- overfriendly endings without speaker basis
- dialect/slang without premise
- trendy meme language outside the speaker's mouth

## josa/eomi as acting
Josa and endings encode distance and pressure:
- 은/는: contrast, topic, self-separation
- 이/가: new information, focus, realization
- 을/를: contact, handling, desire, blame
- 도: residue, concession, "even this"
- 만: minimization, excuse, restriction
- 까지: escalation
- 라니까: insistence
- 잖아: shared excuse or pressure
- 더라/더라고: discovery after the fact
- 버렸어: accident + responsibility
- 맞아: collapse/admission
Choose speech/stance first. Variety is not random decoration.

## subject economy
Do not repeat 나는/내가 unless it creates contrast, blame, confession, distance, or self-recognition. In poetic/minimal modes, first-person can vanish completely if the image carries the stance.

## anti-translationese
Flag:
- excessive nominalizations
- essay endings
- English-like explicit subject chains
- "I feel X because Y" logic
- abstract emotion before concrete evidence
- literal line-by-line translation when singability demands section-level adaptation

Repair:
abstract → object/action/image relation
emotion → body/sensory evidence
theme → speech act / refrain / image return
literal semantics → section-level singable meaning

## image grounding and poetic function
An image must do work:
1. Could this object/world exist in the mode?
2. Does it move, resist, remain, break, cool, heat, stick, vanish, echo, or get handled?
3. Does it alter relation or perception?
4. Can it return with changed meaning?
If not, cut it or make it a texture cue, not a lyric center.

## Korean phoneme and singability
Use:
- open vowels for held peaks where possible: 아/어/오/우/이 by tone
- ㄴ/ㅁ/ㅇ endings for soft resonance
- ㅏ/ㅓ for open belt, ㅣ for thin tension/brightness, ㅜ/오 for round/dark sustain
- plosives/affricates for percussive hooks
- familiar syllable shapes for repeat hooks
- rare/stiff clusters only when the character/genre needs strangeness

For fast songs:
- fewer syllables per beat
- stronger verbs
- less connector clutter
- short hook cells
- planned rests

For slow songs:
- longer clauses allowed
- josa/eomi nuance matters more
- phrase-final lengthening must land on meaningful words

## hook/refrain tests by mode
Conversational hook: a real person could say it three times under pressure.
Poetic refrain: an image/phrase returns with changed meaning.
Chant hook: group can repeat it without semantic fatigue.
Vocable hook: mouth wants to repeat it; vowels and consonants fit groove.
Bilingual hook: cross-language seam sings cleanly.

Reject if the strongest note lands on a weak particle/connector by accident.

## section-ending diversity
Same ending 3+ in a row is a defect unless declared chant/minimal repetition. Ending diversity follows acting:
Verse: observation/discovery
Pre: unfinished pressure
Chorus: claim/refrain/cell
Bridge: rupture/turn
Final: changed claim/image

## plain Korean / image sanity
Remove cues and section labels.
For conversational/narrative: adjacent pairs must survive as plain Korean.
For poetic/symbolic: adjacent pairs must show image relation or deliberate cut.
For surreal: adjacent pairs must obey the internal rule.
For chant/vocable: cells must work in rhythm and not over-explain.

## literature use
Literary quality comes from relation among person, event, background, theme, and object. Do not paste poetic diction. Learn:
- how address creates distance
- how repeated object changes
- how silence carries pressure
- how a final line reframes earlier lines
- how ordinary nouns become charged by placement

## AI failure filter
AI-like Korean appears as:
- concept nouns replacing scene
- perfectly balanced but dead blocks
- decorative metaphors
- clean moral conclusion
- repeated explicit subjects
- section filler
- no embarrassment, friction, silence, or accidental detail
- same lyric birth method every time

Repair by returning to the correct mode, not by adding prettier words.

## functional-morpheme pass
Mark each important line:
- mode function
- listener/target distance
- subject load
- ending job
- peak syllable
- phrase breath
- object/image role

## final acceptance
A Korean lyric passes if:
- it obeys the chosen lyric mode
- a Korean listener can sense the situation/stance/image logic
- repetition has musical or semantic function
- V2/Bridge/Final changes pressure, perception, or sound role
- at least two lines are memorable without generic stock language
- melody can carry the vowels and breath
- cues support performance but do not carry missing story


## CURRENT PATCH — REGISTER STANDARDS, SELF-ANOINTMENT BAN, WORLD CRAFT AXIOMS

### SELF-ANOINTMENT BAN (절대)
어떤 단일 곡도 이 시스템의 품질 표준이 될 수 없다. **특히 이 시스템이 생성한 곡(system-generated song)은 절대 표준이 아니다.** 자기 출력을 표준으로 삼는 순간 (a) 천장이 그 곡 높이로 고정되고 (b) 모든 레지스터가 그 곡의 결·어미·에너지로 끌려가며 (c) 곡간 수렴이 시작된다. 표준은 항상 세 기준의 *교차*다:
① **화자 코퍼스** — 그 캐릭터/페르소나의 기존 곡 2-3 (이 화자의 실제 말).
② **운영자 카탈로그** — operator-history 발췌 + 취향 프로파일 + 1,495곡 분석 원장(이 스튜디오의 검증된 결).
③ **레지스터 매칭 세계 작법** — 아래 공리 + GitHub lyric-craft 풀바디.
모방 순서 ①→②→③, 게이트는 그 *뒤*(WRITE-THEN-CHECK). 충돌 시 ①이 이긴다(화자 진실 > 스튜디오 결 > 일반 작법). 시스템이 막 만든 곡을 "좋은 예시"로 재참조하면 그 자체가 결함.

### WORLD CRAFT AXIOMS (세계 기준 작법 — 평단·거장 합치)
- **"좋은 가사는 캐릭터에서 나온다"** — 지나가는 인물도 히스토리를 갖는다: 편의점 직원이 흡연자면 말보로를 바로 찾고, 비흡연자면 시간이 걸린다 — 그 디테일이 캐릭터다. 작사가는 화자의 단어로만 쓰는 복화술사·스태프지, 작가의 멋을 부리는 사람이 아니다.
- **"가사는 소리의 언어"** — 발음 디자인: 발라드에서 침 튀는 파열음이 핵심 음절에 오면 망한다. 의미와 음가를 동시에 설계. '읽을 눈'을 의식해 리듬을 수비하다 감정 공격을 놓치면 곡이 죽는다.
- **"구체적이면서도 구체적이지 않게"** — 청자가 그림을 그릴 만큼 구체적이되, 사람마다 다른 그림을 그릴 여백을 남긴다(구체성→보편성).
- **사소한 순간이 씨앗** — 샤워기 물줄기 수준의 보잘것없는 구체에서 출발한다. 거대 주제 선언("사랑이란/인생이란")으로 여는 가사는 이미 죽었다.
- **평단 정점의 교훈** — 한국 가사 최고 평가는 말장난·비유·펀치라인 0의 일상 행동 보고체에 갔다(뉴스 끄고 문자 보는 동작 나열만으로 정서 전달). 기교가 아니라 **시선의 정확성**이 천장이다.
- **Pattison 계열** — Object Writing(7감각) / 안정-불안정 prosody / 동사 와트수 / AID(Action·Imagery·Detail). 풀바디는 GitHub lyric-craft.

### REGISTER STANDARDS (레지스터 5종 × 마스터 무브 — 기법 + 원창작 미니 예. 발표곡 가사 인용 절대 금지)
S1에서 레지스터 판정 → 삼중 기준 fetch → 이 곡에 쓸 마스터 무브 2-3개를 INTENT LOCK에 박는다.
- **R1 서정 발라드**: 감정 단어 0 — 그 사람이 그 감정일 때 하는 *행동*으로. 디테일 1개 > 보편 100개. 어미가 온도(~더라 목격회상 / ~는데 미완 여운 / ~지 뭐 체념). 예: "물컵을 두 개 꺼냈다가 / 하나는 도로 넣었어".
- **R2 스토리텔링·리얼리즘**: 카메라 1행 선행(시간·장소), 행동 보고체, 화자가 모르는 것은 남김(전지적 금지). 명사가 동사를 데려온다. 예: "터미널 셋째 줄 의자엔 / 아직 네 자리가 비어 있어".
- **R3 댄스·훅**: 훅=입의 쾌감(모음 착지·반복 도스)+화행 정확히 1개 — 의미 0 금지. 벌스=정보 최소·리듬 최대의 연료. 콜앤리스폰스 자리는 가사 단계에서 선확보.
- **R4 코믹·캐릭터**: 논리의 성실함이 웃음 엔진 — 캐릭터는 진지할수록 웃기다. 어미 변주 필수(앵커+사이 변주), 펀치라인은 정적에 떨어뜨린다.
- **R5 문학·시적**: 이미지 연쇄 허용하되 스파인 1개 필수(난해≠무질서). 모호한 정서일수록 정확한 감각어. 산문 종결(-것이다/-고 있었다) 금지, 행갈이가 호흡.
공통 헌법: 한 행=한 이미지/한 화행 · 서사 레지스터는 인접 2행 평문 복원 · 어미 분포 게이트 · 곡 언어로 구상(번역 금지) · 적대 패스(최약 3행 × 대안 2).
