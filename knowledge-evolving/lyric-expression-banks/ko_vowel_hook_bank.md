# ko_vowel_hook_bank.md — Open-Vowel Hook Targets by BPM (seed)

Hooks live or die on vowel openness at the held note. ㅏ/ㅐ/ㅗ sustain well; ㅡ/ㅣ choke at volume; heavy 받침 (ㄱ/ㅂ/ㄷ) kills sustain mid-hook.

## BPM 70-90 (ballad/slow R&B)
- Hook tail vowels: ㅏ, ㅘ, ㅐ — e.g., "~잖아", "~할까", "남아"
- 받침 budget: ≤1 closed coda per hook line; place on non-held syllables
- Syllables per bar: 4-6 (let the vowel breathe)

## BPM 95-115 (midtempo pop/city pop)
- Hook tail: ㅏ/ㅗ alternation reads bright — "~봐", "~로", "와"
- Syllables per bar: 6-8; sneak ㄴ/ㄹ codas (soft, singable) not ㄱ/ㅂ

## BPM 120-135 (dance/funk-pop)
- Front-load consonant attack, open the tail: "내가 다–", "달라–"
- Repeat economy: 2-4 syllable hook cell ×3 + 1 variant (rule of card 06)
- Syllables per bar: 8-10; chant sections may close codas deliberately for percussive feel

## BPM 140+ (uptempo/EDM-pop)
- One-vowel hooks dominate (아–/오–/예–); lyric meaning carried by pre-hook line
- Avoid ㅢ/ㅟ at held notes entirely

Validation: read the hook ALOUD at tempo before locking — page-reading lies (binding rule, card 05).
