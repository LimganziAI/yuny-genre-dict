# 타투 / Tatoo

## Purpose
Musical vocal palette only. Ignore role/lore unless explicitly requested. Tatoo is female. Sis Tatoo, 시스 타투, 시스타투, Sister Tatoo, and Tatoo are the same character/vocal family, not separate people.

## Aliases
타투, Tatoo, Tattoo, 시스 타투, 시스타투, Sis Tatoo, Sister Tatoo, シスタトゥー

## Core identity
Female dry close-mic vocalist with multiple usable branches. The record chooses the branch that fits the song; do not force one fixed tone across all cases. Low/noir sing-rap is a female Tatoo mode, not male identity.

## Vocal mode A — low/noir rhythmic branch
female low-to-mid dry monotone rhythmic sing-rap, noir close-mic delivery, cool deadpan phrasing, trip-hop/dark-jazz pocket, restrained low register, subtle phrase-end grain

## Vocal mode B — sharp dry high branch
female bright sharp dry soprano-to-high-mezzo edge, cool chic deadpan, biting clear Korean diction, dry close-mic tone, low-effort phrasing, melody stays lifted and intact

## Vocal mode C — fragile light dry branch
female light thin but dry upper-mezzo/soprano tone, delicate but centered, close-mic restraint, unforced small melody, less sweet than Rebecca, less low-heavy than Una

## Korean handle
타투는 여성 보컬이다. Sis Tatoo / 시스타투는 별도 인물이 아니라 타투의 표기/모드다. 낮은 느와르 싱잉랩도 가능하지만 남성 보컬이 아니다. 곡마다 모드를 고른다: 낮고 무심한 리듬/느와르, 밝고 날카로운 high-mezzo/soprano, 여린 dry upper-mezzo/soprano. 멜로디를 떨어뜨리지 않고 보컬 색만 바꾸는 것이 핵심이다.

## Melody firewall
Tatoo changes vocal color and acting only. Preserve topline contour, hook lift, phrase length, chorus shape, pitch center, lyric timing, and section order unless the user explicitly approves melody rebuild.

## Hibiscus case adaptation
For `히비스커스`, use Tatoo Mode B/C hybrid:
- light rainy Korean indie pop skeleton stays
- tone slightly higher than low alto, dry and less sweet
- bright sharp dry soprano-to-high-mezzo or light upper-mezzo anchor helps keep melody from dropping
- close-mic, cool, low-effort, not dragged
- clear biting Korean diction, not elegant legato gloss
- quoted asides such as `"마실래?"` and `"히비스커스"` may use brief narrow-radio color while staying pitched
- cue/rest placement is part of performance, not lyric story
- if render becomes too polished, add dry edge/deadpan diction; do not flatten melody
