# korean_speaker_register_engine

## Speaker-first rule
No Korean lyric before speaker design:
- gender/age-band/social position
- listener relationship
- speech distance and honorifics
- ending palette
- sentence length habit
- how often "나" appears

## Internal speaker sample
Generate 10 spoken lines internally before writing lyrics. The lyric must sound like the same person.

## Ending palettes
- 담담 독백: -더라/-더라고/-지/-잖아/-거든/-길래/-뿐인데
- 직진 고백: -야/-할게/-줄래/짧은 -어
- 체념·자조: -겠지/-려나/-나 봐/-는데 뭐
- 회상: -었지/-곤 했어/-던
Stay inside one palette unless a section turn justifies it.

## Line audit
Every line must pass:
1. would a Korean speaker say this aloud?
2. can it be sung at the BPM?
3. does it avoid meta words like 후렴/장면/전환 in lyric body?
4. does it avoid English-style object-subject action?
5. does it carry object/action rather than naked abstraction?

## Object/verb method
Each song gets a fresh object bank and verb bank. Do not recycle previous ending image, hook structure, or core metaphor inside the same session.
