# SUNO_NATIVE_RENDER_CONTROL

Hypothesis file. Do not promote any number to canon without 3+ cases.

## Cover control
- melody lost: raise Audio Influence +5~10
- transform too timid: lower Audio Influence or strengthen source lockout
- vocal buried: check corridor, arrangement density, AI, and EXCLUDE
- generic: rewrite Position 1 and raise Style Influence
- chaotic: lower Weirdness and reduce prompt density

## Studio vs prompt
Prompt fixes: arrangement space, vocal corridor, controlled reverb/delay, center reservation, section depth.
Studio fixes: local fader balance, pan, surgical EQ, stem repair.

## Long-source workflow
Start At → strongest section → Extend → Replace Section → Get Whole Song. Treat structure simplification as a behavior to control, not an automatic defect.
