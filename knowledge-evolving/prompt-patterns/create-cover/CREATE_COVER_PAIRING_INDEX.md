# CREATE_COVER_PAIRING_INDEX

Use after card 11/14 when CREATE bone and COVER skin need pairing.

## Common pairings
- Organic bone × electronic skin: preserve human timing and breath.
- Sparse bone × dense skin: keep verses sparse, build chorus.
- Vintage bone × modern finish: era lives in CREATE; resolution in COVER.
- Genre-pure × cross-genre cover: substitution map + source lockout.
- Ballad × arena-rock cover: stripped verses, chorus expansion.
- City-pop × modern-funk cover: chords stay; funk affects rhythm and finish.
- Bossa × downtempo cover: dry-close vocal, beat behind.

## Audit
COVER never restates bone. It says what changes, what survives, and how the final record is mixed.
