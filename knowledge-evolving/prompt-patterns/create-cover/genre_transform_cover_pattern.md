# Genre-Transform COVER Pattern

Use when COVER changes the arrangement, genre, groove, instruments, or production world while preserving the CREATE take.

## Required order

```text
target genre micro-anchor
→ preserve map
→ substitution map
→ vocal identity
→ section and energy events
→ full production quality stack
→ final/outro preservation
```

Do not begin a Genre-Transform COVER with generic quality language. The transformation must be audible first.

## Preserve map

State what must survive:

- melody / topline;
- lyric phrasing;
- singer roles;
- duet handoffs;
- section order;
- modulation or micro-bends;
- hook rhythm;
- signature motif;
- good accidents from CREATE.

## Substitution map

State what changes:

- drums: live kit → programmed four-on-floor, 2-step, trap, DnB, etc.;
- bass: bass guitar → 808, mono sub, synth bass, upright bass, etc.;
- harmony bed: guitar/piano → pads, plucks, strings, brass, organ, etc.;
- lead instrument: riff → synth lead, horn stab, guitar articulation, vocal chop, etc.;
- texture: acoustic room → club polish, analog warmth, lo-fi tape, cyber gloss, etc.

## Vocal protection

Dense transformations often bury vocals. Include:

- lead vocal stays forward and human;
- vocal corridor 500Hz-3kHz protected;
- natural breath texture preserved;
- no mechanical edge;
- de-esser region controlled when needed;
- harmony stack supports, not covers, the lead.

## Audio Influence guide

- 60-75: preserve song identity strongly.
- 45-55: balanced transformation.
- 20-40: texture-forward transformation.

Use higher values when melody, phrasing, or duet roles are precious.

## Failure risks

- too much target genre erases the hook;
- too little substitution sounds like the old version with new adjectives;
- quality language before genre anchor creates repair-mode average output;
- dense arrangement buries vocal;
- percussion grid fights the source phrasing.

## Reusable COVER skeleton

```text
[Target microgenre/era] at [BPM/feel], preserving the original topline, lyric phrasing, section order, hook rhythm, and signature motif from the CREATE take. Replace [old drum/bass/harmony/lead texture] with [new drum/bass/harmony/lead texture], keeping the vocal identity [voice spec] forward and human throughout. Verse remains [energy], chorus becomes [energy], bridge [event], final chorus [event]. Protect vocal corridor 500Hz-3kHz, mono sub 20-80Hz, separated kick/bass center, controlled 200-400Hz mud, smooth 2-5kHz high-mids, controlled 8-14kHz air, stereo instrument placement, controlled reverb/delay tails, transient punch, glue compression, light tape/tube saturation. Preserve outro timing and final hook ending.
```
