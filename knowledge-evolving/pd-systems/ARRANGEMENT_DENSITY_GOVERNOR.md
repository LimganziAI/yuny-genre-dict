# ARRANGEMENT_DENSITY_GOVERNOR

## Section density
- Verse: 3-4 simultaneous layers max; vocal lane visible.
- Pre: add one tension layer or percussion motion.
- Chorus: hook-support layers, space kept for lead vocal.
- Bridge: one harmonic or texture turn only.
- Final Chorus: exactly one new lift.
- Outro: signature motif maintained, not dumped.

## Vocal survival
Thin/light vocal: remove midrange clutter, call-response gaps.  
Thick vocal: low-mid room, contrast before peaks.  
Speech-tone: rhythmic space and dry presence.  
Belt: runway into peak, no dense consonants on sustain.
