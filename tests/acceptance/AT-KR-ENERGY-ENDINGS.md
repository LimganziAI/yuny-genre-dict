# AT-KR-ENERGY-ENDINGS

## purpose
Check song-level intensity and Korean line-ending cadence before final Suno packaging.

## required checks
- section energy changes intentionally
- V2 adds angle, not only volume
- Final changes meaning, stance, energy, or delivery
- important exposed lines have singable endings
- cues improve performance but do not carry missing story
- lyric still works when cues are removed

## ending cadence labels
- hard stop
- soft landing
- open tail
- unfinished tail
- spoken turn
- chant anchor

## repair order
1. remap section energy
2. vary line endings
3. move directness to the right section
4. adjust hook ending for vocal carry
5. place cues
6. micro-edit only cue-exposed endings or breath lengths
7. reread without cues

Active v5 candidate.
