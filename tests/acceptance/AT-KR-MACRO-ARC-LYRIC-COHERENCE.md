# AT-KR-MACRO-ARC-LYRIC-COHERENCE

## purpose
Check whether a Korean lyric works as a whole song, not only as separate natural lines.

This test protects against lyrics that are locally understandable but globally incoherent.

## trigger
Use for every Korean SONG-FULL, STAGED-FULL, LYRIC-CRAFT, LYRIC-REPAIR, or serious Korean lyric complaint.

## required pre-draft map
Before lyric drafting, establish:

```text
SONG THESIS:
LISTENER JOURNEY:
MACRO ARC:
- Opening:
- V1:
- Pre:
- Chorus:
- V2:
- Bridge:
- Final:
- Outro:
SECTION MODE MAP:
```

## core judgment
A good Korean lyric must pass three levels:

1. sentence level: each line and adjacent pair is understandable Korean
2. section level: each section has a clear job
3. song level: the whole song moves somewhere

## pass criteria
- The listener can summarize the song movement in one sentence.
- V1 gives a grounded situation, not scattered objects.
- Pre raises pressure from the situation.
- Chorus contains the memory center: a phrase, sentence, or sound-cell worth repeating.
- V2 adds information, changes the angle, or reveals a deeper reason.
- Bridge changes the speaker's defense, confession, or frame.
- Final chorus is not only louder; it changes meaning, stance, harmony, or production function.
- Outro leaves an action, image, sound, or residue that belongs to this song.
- Metaphor grows from the scene instead of being pasted as decorative poetry.
- Creative expression remains understandable without becoming flat explanation.

## fail criteria
- Individual lines can be understood but the song has no movement.
- Sections repeat the same idea without pressure change.
- Objects appear because they were in the premise, not because the speaker notices them.
- The lyric explains the thesis instead of making the listener experience it.
- Metaphors are pretty but detached from the scene.
- The final chorus repeats the first chorus without changed meaning.
- Cue or arrangement creates energy that the lyric has not earned.

## repair order
Patch in this order:
1. song thesis
2. macro arc
3. section job
4. lyric mode by section
5. line language
6. metaphor and image
7. hook sound/shape
8. cue and production packaging

## Suno cross-check
After lyric passes, CREATE/COVER must be checked separately:
- CREATE under 1000 chars, positive execution descriptors
- COVER under 1000 chars, clear preserve/change map
- LYRIC plus cues under 5000 chars
- EXCLUDE carries avoid-list items
- slider values fit goal
