# AT-KR-LYRIC-CRAFT-QUALITY-PASS

## purpose
Judge Korean lyric writing quality before Suno packaging.

This test focuses on composition as writing: situation, speaker posture, scene-built metaphor, vocabulary, section movement, hook identity, and final shift.

## trigger
Use for Korean LYRIC-CRAFT, SONG-FULL, STAGED-FULL, LYRIC-REPAIR, and any Korean lyric quality complaint.

## source priority
1. User/team corpus baseline
2. Current user premise and references
3. K-pop corpus calibration for repetition, hook, and section compression
4. General lyric craft knowledge

Do not copy source lyrics. Extract craft behavior.

## required pre-draft craft brief
```text
SONG AS A SCENE:

V2 ANGLE CHANGE:

VOCABULARY PALETTE:
LINE LOGIC:
```

## quality criteria
A Korean lyric passes only when:

1. The situation is recoverable from the lyric.
2. The speaker posture is visible in wording and action.
3. Important objects are acted on, handled, avoided, worn, heard, checked, or noticed.
4. The chorus has an identity: confession, contradiction, slogan, image, sound-cell, body action, or character catchphrase.
5. V2 changes angle through new evidence, space, object, social relation, body response, or cause.
6. The final section changes stance, address, verb, object, harmony, delivery, or arrangement function.
7. Metaphor grows from the available scene.
8. Vocabulary has a coherent palette instead of random poetic nouns.
9. Friendly/plain lines and poetic lines are balanced according to speaker and section.
10. The lyric can be reverse-briefed into a plausible original prompt.

## fail criteria
- Correct Korean sentences but no song movement.
- Scene objects appear without verbs or attention.
- Chorus explains the theme instead of becoming memorable.
- V2 repeats V1 pressure.
- Final repeats without earned reason.
- Metaphor is decorative and not traceable to scene.
- Vocabulary palette jumps between unrelated worlds.
- Lines sound like translated prompt fragments.
- The lyric feels written to satisfy rules, not to become a song.
