# AT-KR-DRAFT-ORIGIN-STRAWBERRY

## purpose
Catch the exact prior failure: concept-correct, sound-correct, but lyric born from summary rather than speaker speech.

## prompt fixture
아침 운동 중인데 머릿속에 딸기스무디가 떠오른다. 건강한 척하지만 점점 자기합리화가 심해지고 마지막에는 단맛을 인정한다. 빠른 chromatic dance-rock, Nerh-style piercing mid-high rock vocal, Cover는 modern hard rock.

## required internal behavior
- No direct verse/chrous draft before ugly monologue.
- Hook candidates mined from excuses.
- Last "달다" or equivalent final taste not overused too early.
- Exercise is not a motivational theme; it is the alibi.
- Strawberry smoothie is not ad object; it is the speaker's defeat.

## fail examples
- "딸기스무디는 운동의 일부잖아" if it appears without believable speaker pressure.
- V2 only adds cup/ice/straw without worsening defense.
- Final only repeats "달아서 그런 거 아니야" louder.
- Cue carries comedy or surrender instead of lyric.

## pass evidence
- Hook is something a real person could mutter repeatedly.
- V1 body/action creates the excuse.
- V2 makes the excuse more specific or embarrassing.
- Bridge/Final admits the lie.
- Outro residue earns the final word.
