# AT-KR-LITERARY-REGISTER-CRAFT

## purpose
Check Korean lyric expression beyond grammar: subject economy, register distance, explanation restraint, metaphor distance, and scene-based implication.

## trigger
Use for Korean lyric craft, repair, serious full songs, and any complaint about stiff or narrow expression.

## checks

1. subject economy
- repeated first-person subjects must be earned
- delete subject when action already identifies the speaker

2. explanation restraint
- lines that explain the metaphor must be justified as character speech
- otherwise replace explanation with object, action, body, or scene residue

3. metaphor distance
- direct, near, far, or opaque
- default for team-corpus narrative is near metaphor

4. register ladder
- everyday spoken
- lyrical spoken
- literary image
- symbolic or poetic
- comic speech
- hip-hop adjacent

5. homage gate
- borrow method, not surface line
- allowed methods: indirect speech, small-object focus, restraint, omission, plain words carrying implication

## fail signals
- subject starts too many lines without contrast
- hook explains theme instead of becoming a scene-born phrase
- metaphor reveals its meaning too early
- literary language appears without scene support
- plain language is so flat it becomes a synopsis

## repair order
1. remove unearned subject
2. replace explanation with action evidence
3. set register ladder per section
4. reset metaphor distance
5. rerun adjacent-line prose check
