# KOREAN_PHONEME_SINGABILITY_GATE

## principle
Korean singing quality is not only syllable count. It is breath, phonological phrase, vowel/coda choice, familiar syllable shapes, and placement of meaning words.

## line break
Line break should follow:
- breath unit
- phonological phrase
- meaning pressure
- melodic cell
not arbitrary visual symmetry.

## peak placement
On held or peak notes, prefer:
- meaning-bearing words
- sustainable vowels
- emotionally useful endings
Avoid accidental peaks on weak particles/connectors unless deliberately stylized.

## vowel color
- ㅏ/ㅓ: open belt, direct admission, big release
- ㅗ/ㅜ: round, dark, contained, smoother sustain
- ㅣ: thin, tense, bright, piercing, anxious
- ㅐ/ㅔ: conversational brightness, quick turns

## coda/cut
- ㄴ/ㅁ/ㅇ soften endings and sustain better
- ㄱ/ㄷ/ㅂ/ㅈ/ㅊ can give rhythmic bite
- heavy coda clusters can choke a long note
- final consonant difficulty may be used for strain only when wanted

## fast BPM
At 120+ BPM:
- reduce connector clutter
- prefer short predicate/action cells
- keep hook mouthable
- avoid long nominal phrases

## slow BPM
At slower tempos:
- longer Korean clauses are possible
- preserve josa/eomi nuance
- let phrase-final lengthening carry emotional turn

## final check
Mark hook syllables:
word / vowel / coda / meaning weight / repeat comfort.
If the strongest syllable is musically weak or linguistically meaningless, rewrite the hook.
