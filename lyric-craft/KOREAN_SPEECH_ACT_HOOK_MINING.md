# KOREAN_SPEECH_ACT_HOOK_MINING

## thesis
A Korean hook should be a repeatable utterance under pressure, not a topic sentence.

## speech act inventory
Denial, defense, bargaining, teasing, refusal, confession, accusation, self-mockery, surrender, dare, promise, regret, avoidance, correction, pretending-not-to-care.

## tests
- Can a real person say this three times?
- Is it shorter than the explanation it replaces?
- Does it imply a situation without naming the whole theme?
- Can Final Chorus change its meaning?
- Does the core word sing?

## common failures
"운동의 일부잖아" = maybe clever, but can be too composed.
"목마른 거라니까" = speech act, better.
"나 이 정도는 마셔도 되잖아" = defense + repeatable.
"먹겠다는 말은 안 했어" = denial + contradiction.

## output use
The final lyric does not need to show all candidates. The system must use candidates internally to avoid dead hooks.
