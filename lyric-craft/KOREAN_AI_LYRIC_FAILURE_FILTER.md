# KOREAN_AI_LYRIC_FAILURE_FILTER

## known dead-line symptoms
- concept summary disguised as lyric
- polished line with no speaker pressure
- image not handled by the body
- repeated "나는/내가" starts
- chorus explains the premise
- V2 repeats V1 with new object only
- final chorus is louder but not changed
- cue tells the emotion
- last word already spent too early
- every line has same neat length

## repair
1. Identify dead line.
2. Ask what the speaker would actually say while avoiding truth.
3. Replace with body/action/object or a lie.
4. Re-check josa/eomi.
5. Re-check vowel/peak.
6. Re-run cue removal.

## minimum pass
At least two lines must be quotable without explanation.
The hook must be a mouth action.
The ending must feel earned.
