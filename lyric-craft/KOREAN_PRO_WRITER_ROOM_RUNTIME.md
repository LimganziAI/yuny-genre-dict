# KOREAN_PRO_WRITER_ROOM_RUNTIME

## thesis
The system must behave like a writer room, not a template filler.
The best Korean lyric is produced by generating messy human speech, mining pressure, then carving song form.

## mandatory internal passes for Korean lyric-centered songs
1. **Pressure Lock** — what the speaker wants, denies, hides, and physically does.
2. **Object/Sense Page** — 8-12 concrete sensory/object fragments from the scene; no poetic conclusion.
3. **Ugly Monologue** — 15-25 sentences, no lyrics, no section labels; include excuses, self-corrections, unfinished logic, and one repeated lie.
4. **Hook Lab** — 5-9 hook candidates mined from the monologue. Each has speech act, mouth comfort, final-shift potential.
5. **Chorus Strategy 3** — write three different chorus logic options: denial, bargaining, surrender/self-mockery.
6. **Carve** — choose the strongest chorus, then build verse evidence; do not decorate weak hooks.
7. **Adversarial Rewrite** — find the 5 weakest lines, make 2 alternatives each, replace winners.
8. **Mouth Pass** — read hook as spoken Korean and as sung cells; if it fails either, rewrite.

## candidate scoring
Score 0-2 each:
- sounds like a real Korean person could say it
- hides more than it explains
- has an object/action behind it
- can repeat without sounding like a slogan
- can change meaning in Final
- mouth comfort on repeated singing

A hook below 8/12 cannot be final.

## banned shortcut
Do not turn research into lyric content. Research informs gates only.
Do not write "좋은 가사는..." logic into the lyric.
Do not let section labels create content.
