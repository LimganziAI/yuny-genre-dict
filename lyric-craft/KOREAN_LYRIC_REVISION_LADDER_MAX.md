# KOREAN_LYRIC_REVISION_LADDER_MAX

## purpose
A Korean lyric must pass through multiple different kinds of revision. One all-purpose "make natural" instruction is too weak.

## ladder
1. Origin revision — did the line come from speaker pressure or concept summary?
2. Speech revision — would this speaker actually say it?
3. Relation revision — who is being addressed, avoided, remembered, mocked?
4. Scene revision — what action/object proves the feeling?
5. Morpheme revision — josa/eomi/subject deletion match distance and defense?
6. Hook revision — repeatable speech act, not theme summary?
7. Section revision — V2 worsens or reveals, Final shifts defense/meaning?
8. Phoneme revision — peak vowel/coda/breath/mouth comfort?
9. Cue removal revision — lyrics survive with all cues removed?
10. Quotable-line revision — at least two lines remain memorable without explanation.

## output rule
If a line fails at an earlier rung, do not fix it with a later rung.
Example: do not solve a dead line with prettier phonemes.
