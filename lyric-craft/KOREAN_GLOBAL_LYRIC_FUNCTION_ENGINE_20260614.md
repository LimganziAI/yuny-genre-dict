This engine is not an artist-imitation layer. It turns a broad craft-diversity pool into original lyric rules.

## 1. Master goal
YUNY should write lyrics by first deciding what the lyric must DO in the record:
speaker/listener relation · scene/object bank · thought spine · hook function · section jobs · prosody/mouthfeel · final residue.

## 2. Universal gate
A serious lyric must pass these before cues:
1. speaker pressure is clear.
2. listener/addressee is recoverable.
3. concrete carrier appears before abstract emotion.
4. vocabulary attaches to action/body/place/time/social pressure.
5. hook is a speech act or mouth action.
6. V2/Bridge/Final change the meaning or pressure.
7. final line leaves residue, not a moral summary.
8. cue removal still leaves a song.

## 3. Vocabulary attachment patterns
- object + action: hold, fold, leave, count, hide, return, erase, spill.
- body + withheld speech: throat, hand, eyes, shoulder, breath, knees.
- time + object: dawn, winter, old room, bus time, album, receipt, window.
- place + relation: station, stairs, kitchen, road, door, stage, seat.
- social pressure + wound: name, rumor, score, promise, number, text, debt.
- sound + gesture: rain, bell, applause, shoe step, guitar stroke, closing door.
- abstraction after evidence: love/regret/hope/anger/loneliness only after carriers.

## 4. Korean operating engine

### Josa acting
은/는 = contrast, distance, self-separation.
이/가 = focus, new realization, who-did-it.
을/를 = handling, desire, blame.
도 = residue, concession, even-this.
만 = narrowing, excuse, scarcity.
까지 = escalation.
조차/마저 = despair, last remaining thing also failing.

### Eomi temperature
-다 = flat fact, distance.
-네 = noticed realization.
-더라 = witnessed memory.
-거든 = intimate justification.
-잖아 = shared knowledge as pressure/excuse.
-지 = acceptance or defensive closure.
-는데 = suspended pressure.
-ㄹ게 = promise to the listener.
noun-ending = freeze-frame, residue, cut.

### Sound/prosody
Open vowels ㅏ/ㅗ/ㅐ lift and belt; closed vowels ㅡ/ㅣ restrain/tighten.
Stop codas ㄱ/ㄷ/ㅂ/ㅈ/ㅊ hit; nasal/liquid ㄴ/ㅁ/ㅇ/ㄹ sustain.
Internal rhyme must serve pressure, not decoration.

## 5. Genre lane engines

### Hip-hop / rap
Job: claim, revision, accusation, status cost.
Rule: line-end nouns carry claims; next line revises or wounds the claim.
Failure: rhyme density with no thought; empty flex.

### R&B / soul
Job: intimate body/room pressure.
Rule: attach every feeling to breath, light, touch, temperature, silence, phone, door.
Failure: late-night mood with no relation pressure.

### Ballad
Job: delayed confession, soft guilt, memory object, final residue.
Rule: one concrete object returns changed across sections.
Failure: mature philosophy without object/action.

### Folk / indie / literary
Job: plain witness, small object, quiet turn.
Rule: three small objects before the first big feeling.
Failure: diary dump, essay conclusion, decorative metaphor fog.

### Rock / band
Job: body voltage, declaration, collective release.
Rule: peak line must carry open vowel + body/action/place, not abstract slogan.
Failure: message louder than scene.

### K-pop / dance
Job: mouth-action hook and performance cell.
Rule: choose hook by mouth action first: chant, snap, call-response, whisper, vowel lift.
Failure: meaning-first hook that does not move in the mouth.

### Trot / public song
Job: public address and shared emotion.
Rule: refrain must sound like an original saying, not a copied proverb.
Failure: museum diction or private monologue.

### 7080 / legacy
Job: followable story and durable image.
Rule: plain narrative arc, anchored to season/place/time.
Failure: period objects without current speaker.

### Modern life folk
Job: ordinary object becomes emotional proof.
Rule: mundane object only works when it carries hidden pressure.
Failure: cute everyday list with no turn.

## 6. Japanese/global extension

### Japanese
Register first, mora second, meaning third.
Count mora; long vowels, small っ, and ん consume time.
Use omission, image-return, particle stance, and residue. Do not force Korean explanation logic.

### English/global
Stress-timed. Content words on strong beats. Hook/title payload on stressed syllable.
Use rhyme family intentionally. No literal translation of Korean idiom or ending temperature.

### Bilingual
Switch at section seams or hook events. Keep one emotional home language. Transfer section function, not line-by-line meaning.

## 7. Hook function menu
command · denial · confession · vow · accusation · consolation-command · protest · self-defense · chant · call-response · image-return · private aside · sound-cell · public address.

## 8. Section completion map
Verse 1 = evidence/world.
Pre = pressure and suspension.
Chorus = hook function.
Verse 2 = new evidence or contradiction.
Bridge = truth crack / new addressee / frame break.
Final chorus = same hook re-means after bridge.
Outro = residue object/sound/body gesture.

## 9. Acceptance quickcheck
- Does a concrete carrier precede abstraction?
- Can you name the listener?
- Does the hook do a speech act or mouth action?
- Does V2 add something V1 did not?
- Does Bridge crack the frame?
- Does Final re-mean or leave residue?
- Do josa/eomi choices change stance?
- Does the text survive without cues?
