# KR Lyric Mode Router V4.2 RC

Purpose: prevent one-method Korean lyric generation.

## choose mode first
conversational/narrative · poetic/symbolic · surreal/artful · chant/anthem · vocable/phonetic · bilingual · theatrical/character · minimal/fragment

## method is mode-bound
Ugly monologue is strong for conversational pressure, comedy, denial, shame, confession, and ordinary speech.
It is not mandatory for poetry, chant, vocable, surreal, theatrical, or minimal work.

## universal non-copy rule
Uploaded poems, lyrics, corpora, translations, and user history supply craft functions only:
image pressure, return, silence, speech repair, repeat dose, section alignment, phoneme comfort, cue placement, and failure filters.
Never paste source lines.

## final test
Does the lyric succeed by its locked mode, not by a generic AI lyric checklist?
