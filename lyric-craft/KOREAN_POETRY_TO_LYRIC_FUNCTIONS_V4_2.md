# Korean Poetry / Literature → Lyric Functions V4.2

Use literature as a craft lens:
- image system: object returns with changed emotional charge
- silence: what is omitted creates pressure
- line cut: break creates thought turn or breath
- background: place/time shapes relation, not decoration
- persona: speaking position controls diction
- residue: ending object/word remains after the thesis is gone

Never imitate or quote protected literary lines. Convert to mechanisms only.
