# CUE_AFTER_LYRIC_RENDER_ORDER

## PRINCIPLE
Lyric and cue are not generated together. The lyric must stand first. `[ ]` cue and `* *` performance marks are added only after strip-read pass.

## ORDER
1. raw monologue
2. hook mining
3. lyric carving
4. meaning / V2 / Final evolution
5. spoken register repair
6. breath/grid compression
7. strip-read test: lyric works with zero cues
8. cue pass: structure cue → vocal acting cue → color cue
9. final count: lyrics+cues combined <=5000

## CUE RULES
- Cues describe HOW, never WHAT.
- [Singing:] is section vocal state, not story.
- [Whispered] only after silence/drop and on short line.
- [Held] only line-final open vowel; 1-2 per song.
- Tier-S structural events are strongest: [Instrumental:], [Band drops out], [Sudden drop], [One-bar rest], [Harmony enters].
- Device count >=4 and <= lines/3.
- `* *` is weak decorative emphasis; never the main controller.

## FAILURES
- cue explains missing emotion
- cue is more important than lyric
- every line has cue
- cue sits at section end instead of between the exact lines it affects
