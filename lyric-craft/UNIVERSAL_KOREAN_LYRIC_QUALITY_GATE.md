# Universal Korean Lyric Quality Gate — 범용 가사 품질 엔진

Purpose: prevent Korean lyrics from sliding into generic AI text. This is not a character-only route. It is the default writer-room for any Korean song.

## Lock before lines
- speaker: who is speaking, what they would not say
- situation: what pressure exists now
- scene/object bank: concrete objects, body actions, time/weather/material
- thought spine: 4-7 arrows of movement
- hook function: speech-act, image return, chant cell, sound cell, bilingual seam

## Naked lyric test
Remove all cues. The lyric must still:
- have at least two memorable lines
- show concrete action/object before abstraction
- change pressure/perception in V2/Bridge/Final
- sing in Korean without awkward subject chains or connector clutter
- avoid theme summary, pretty abstraction stacks, generic uplift, decorative metaphor fog, and slot-filler lines

## Production use
After the lyric passes:
- CREATE LYRIC becomes source-performance runway: breath, timing, hook cell, source cue obedience, motif handoff.
- COVER LYRIC becomes final-record staging runway: density, whisper pickup, hard stop, drop cell, no-vocal fill, final hold/cut.

The goal is not to quote great lyrics. The goal is to turn craft principles into fresh, singable, source-to-final Suno records.
